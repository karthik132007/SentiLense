How sentiment works +

The SVM + RoBERTa fusion model estimates negative, neutral, and positive tone. Neutral is a trained class.

Text and general webpages use a learned fusion of SVM and Cardiff Twitter RoBERTa. Movie reviews use the binary IMDb SVM, where neutral means uncertainty. Results can miss sarcasm or context.
